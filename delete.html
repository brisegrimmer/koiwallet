Brisanje naloga i podataka — Koi Wallet

Poslednja izmena: 20. oktobar 2026.

U aplikaciji (najbrže)

  1. Otvori Koi Wallet i idi na Podešavanja.
  2. Dodirni svoj Nalog.
  3. Izaberi Obriši nalog i potvrdi (lozinkom ili Google prijavom).

Time se odmah brišu tvoj nalog, svi unosi, ciljevi, planovi i podešavanja sa servera i sa telefona.

Bez aplikacije

Ako si deinstalirao aplikaciju ili više ne možeš da se prijaviš, pošalji zahtev na hukicnizar@gmail.com sa e-poštom naloga. Nalog i podatke brišemo u roku od 30 dana i potvrđujemo odgovorom.

Šta se briše

  • Nalog (e-pošta, ime, način prijave) u Firebase Authentication.
  • Svi unosi, ciljevi štednje, plan meseca, kategorije i podešavanja u Firestore bazi.
  • Zajednički prostor sa partnerom: ako si ti vlasnik, brišu se zajednički unosi, ciljevi i pozivni kodovi, a partner zadržava svoj nalog i svoje lične unose. Ako si partner, izlaziš iz domaćinstva, a tvoji zajednički unosi se uklanjaju iz zajedničkog prostora.

Šta može da ostane

Rezervne kopije servera mogu da zadrže ostatke najviše 30 dana, posle čega nestaju. Podatke ne čuvamo iz drugih razloga.

Rad bez naloga

Ako nikad nisi pravio nalog, podaci su samo na tvom telefonu i nestaju kad obrišeš aplikaciju (ili u Podešavanjima obrišeš podatke).

Delete your account and data — Koi Wallet

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Koi Wallet and go to Settings.
  2. Tap your Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm (with your password or Google sign-in).

This immediately deletes your account, all entries, goals, plans and settings from the server and from your phone.

Without the app

If you uninstalled the app or can no longer sign in, email hukicnizar@gmail.com with your account email. We delete the account and data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • The account (email, name, sign-in method) in Firebase Authentication.
  • All entries, savings goals, monthly plan, categories and settings in the Firestore database.
  • The shared space with your partner: if you are the owner, the shared entries, goals and invitation codes are deleted, and your partner keeps their own account and personal entries. If you are the partner, you leave the household and your shared entries are removed from the shared space.

What may remain

Server backups may hold remnants for up to 30 days, after which they disappear. We keep no data for any other reason.

Using the app without an account

If you never created an account, your data is only on your phone and disappears when you uninstall the app (or clear its data in Settings).
